About Us

ServiceHub is a platform designed to make finding reliable service professionals simple, convenient, and accessible.

From electrical repairs to carpentry, plumbing, cleaning, appliance repair, and painting, ServiceHub brings everyday service needs together in one place.

Our goal is to connect customers with skilled professionals while making the process of finding a service, requesting assistance, and managing a booking easier.

We believe professional services should be easy to find, straightforward to request, and convenient to manage.
