About Us

About ServiceHub

ServiceHub is a platform designed to make finding reliable service professionals simple, convenient, and accessible.

From electrical repairs to carpentry, plumbing, cleaning, appliance repair, and painting, ServiceHub brings everyday service needs together in one place.

Our goal is to connect customers with skilled professionals while making the process of finding a service, requesting assistance, and managing a booking easier.

We believe professional services should be easy to find, straightforward to request, and convenient to manage.

Terms & Conditions

Terms governing the use of ServiceHub by users and workers

1. Terms and Conditions for Users

  • Users must provide accurate, complete, and up-to-date information when creating and using their ServiceHub account.
  • A user may have a maximum of three active bookings at a time.
  • A user cannot create another active booking for a service that already has an active booking under their account.
  • If no worker accepts a booking within 30 minutes, the booking will be marked as NO WORKER and the user may request the service again.
  • If a worker has accepted a booking, the user must contact ServiceHub for cancellation or assistance instead of attempting to cancel the booking directly.
  • Cancellation charges may apply when a worker has already accepted a booking and has started travelling toward the service location.
  • If a worker has started the service, charges may apply for the work, travel, and approved parts or materials already used, even if the service is later cancelled or cannot be completed.
  • Users must provide accurate and relevant information about the requested service and clearly describe the issue or work required.
  • Users must provide the worker with reasonable access to the service location and cooperate as reasonably required for the service to be performed.
  • Users must confirm completion of the service after the worker marks the service as completed. If the user does not respond within the specified confirmation period, the booking may be automatically completed.
  • An additional charge may apply when a booking is automatically completed because the user does not provide the required completion confirmation within the specified time.
  • Users who contact ServiceHub for assistance before automatic completion may have their booking reviewed by ServiceHub administration.
  • Users are responsible for paying the final amount applicable to their completed or otherwise billable service.
  • Users may be asked to approve additional parts or materials required during a service before those items are added to the final bill.
  • Users may provide a rating and predefined feedback for a worker after a service has been completed or automatically completed.
  • Users must treat workers respectfully and must not use ServiceHub for unlawful, abusive, threatening, fraudulent, or inappropriate purposes.
  • ServiceHub may review complaints, disputes, cancellations, and other exceptional situations and may take appropriate action when necessary.

2. Terms and Conditions for Workers

  • Workers must provide accurate, genuine, and up-to-date information during registration and verification.
  • Only verified workers are permitted to accept service bookings.
  • Workers must keep their availability information accurate and up to date.
  • A worker may have only one active service booking at a time.
  • When a worker accepts a booking, they are expected to proceed with the service and communicate with the customer as reasonably required.
  • Workers may reject or decline pending bookings when they are unable or unwilling to provide the requested service.
  • Workers must only accept bookings for services that they are qualified and registered to provide.
  • Workers must mark a service as started only after reaching the customer's location and actually beginning the work.
  • Workers must accurately report the progress and completion status of the service through the ServiceHub platform.
  • If a worker is unable to complete a service after starting it, the worker must provide an appropriate reason through the platform and inform the customer as required.
  • Workers must not intentionally delay, abandon, or misuse an active service booking.
  • Workers may be required to provide information about parts or materials required for a service, and additional parts or materials may require customer approval.
  • Worker location may be collected while using relevant ServiceHub services in order to support service coordination, worker availability, distance-based service features, and customer safety.
  • Workers must keep location information and other service-related information accurate whenever such information is required by ServiceHub.
  • After completing the work, workers must mark the service as completed through the ServiceHub platform.
  • Workers must remain available for the current booking until the booking has been completed, automatically completed, or otherwise resolved by ServiceHub.
  • Workers must behave professionally and respectfully toward customers and must not misuse customer information or the ServiceHub platform.
  • Workers must not use ServiceHub customer information for purposes unrelated to providing the requested service.
  • ServiceHub may review worker performance, ratings, complaints, cancellations, and other service-related activity.
  • ServiceHub may take appropriate action, including restricting or suspending a worker's access to the platform, when necessary.

3. General Terms

  • Service availability depends on the availability of suitable workers in the requested service area.
  • ServiceHub may contact users or workers when necessary to resolve booking issues, cancellations, disputes, payment matters, or service-related concerns.
  • ServiceHub may review booking and service information when investigating complaints, disputes, fraud, misuse, or other exceptional situations.
  • Payments, applicable service charges, cancellation charges, and other billable amounts must be settled according to the information presented to the user before or after the service, as applicable.
  • Payment failures do not necessarily cancel a completed service, and users may be required to retry a failed payment.
  • Refunds, where applicable, are subject to review and approval by ServiceHub.
  • ServiceHub may update these Terms & Conditions when the platform, services, or applicable policies change.
  • By continuing to use ServiceHub, users and workers agree to comply with these Terms & Conditions and any applicable ServiceHub policies.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

When you use ServiceHub, we may collect information required to create and manage your account and provide our services, including account information, contact details, service requests, booking information, and information related to interactions with the platform.

Depending on the service and features you use, ServiceHub may also collect service-related location information. For example, worker location may be collected while relevant worker services are active to support service coordination, availability, distance-based features, and customer safety.

How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, process service requests, facilitate communication, manage bookings, coordinate services, process payments, provide customer and worker support, manage feedback and ratings, improve the ServiceHub platform, and maintain platform security.

Location information, where collected, may be used for service coordination, worker availability, distance-related service features, and safety-related purposes.

Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide requested services, connect customers and workers, operate the platform, process payments, provide support, comply with applicable legal requirements, investigate misuse or disputes, or protect users and ServiceHub.

Location Information

When location-related features are required for a service, ServiceHub may collect and use location information to support the operation of those features.

Worker location may be used to support service coordination, availability, distance-based service features, and customer safety. Location information should only be used for legitimate ServiceHub-related purposes.

Information Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with your account against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

ServiceHub may retain account, booking, payment, service, feedback, and related information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, meet operational requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Your Choices and Rights

You may be able to access, update, or request changes to information associated with your account, subject to applicable requirements and platform functionality.

If you have questions or concerns about your personal information or how it is used, you may contact ServiceHub using the available contact information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

ServiceHub may update this Privacy Policy when the platform, services, data practices, or applicable requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
